# 0005. Tiered Counterpart Routing, Async Dispatch Lease, and Invariant Retirement

Status: accepted

Real-world `/ale` runs on `line-bot-waifu` and `antigravity-skills` exposed four operational bottlenecks: (1) new host models (`Orchestrator`, `Gemini 4 Carbon`, `Gemini 4 Hydrogen`) were unrecognized by `ledger.py` and every Gemini review consumed scarce `claude-opus-5-5-high` third-party quota regardless of task tier; (2) `model_router.py --role review` exceeded the 10-second synchronous command wait and moved to the background, causing the `Stop` hook in `pretool_gate.py` to block turn completion and force `sleep` polling loops; (3) Cycle-2 `Strike-Breaker` runs lacked project-level `LESSONS_LEARNED.md` context and were blocked by `PreToolUse` after `friction` reset `ll_gate_passed`; and (4) `red_gate.py` lacked file-scoped regex checks (`path :: pattern`) and a retirement mechanism for superseded architectural invariants. We decided to: (1) map all 22 host models with Tiered Counterpart Routing (`claude-sonnet-5-5-high` for Tier 2 Gemini reviews, `claude-opus-5-5-high` for Tier 3 reviews and Cycle-2 `strike-breaker`, `gemini-4-argon-high` / `gemini-4-carbon-high` for Claude authors, `gemini-4-hydrogen-high` for `fast-exec`, and automatic cross-family fallback chains); (2) record a time-bounded `dispatch_in_progress` lease in `ledger.json` during cross-model calls so the `Stop` hook yields cleanly for background completion and auto-transitions verified ledgers to `status: "verified"`; (3) inject project-level `LESSONS_LEARNED.md` into `review` and `strike-breaker` prompts, raise diff capture to `80 KB` including untracked files, and scope subprocess hook permissions via `ALE_SUBPROCESS_ROLE`; and (4) add `path :: pattern` support in `red_gate.py` alongside `ledger.py retire-ll` (`Status: Superseded by <ID>`).
